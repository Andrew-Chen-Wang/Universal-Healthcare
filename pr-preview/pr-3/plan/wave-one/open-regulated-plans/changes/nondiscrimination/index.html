Open the regulated plans

Current draft: Germany-style hybrid

Non-discrimination by participating providers

expandedYear 1Federal insurance & health law
Today

ACA Section 1557 and civil rights law apply unevenly; providers can refuse patients by insurance type.

Under the plan

Plans and providers may not discriminate by plan type in emergencies and urgent care.

Trade-off

Limits how much faster employer-plan patients can be seen for urgent care.
